## Full description (up to 4000 characters)

Ось (Os) is a business browser for people who juggle several accounts and care about privacy. Personal mail, work tools, shopping and social networks live in separate profiles that can't see each other. Ось has no servers, no ads and no analytics: your data stays on your phone.

ISOLATED PROFILES
• Up to 50 profiles, each with its own sign-ins, cookies and tabs
• Two Gmail inboxes or two work accounts at the same time — no signing out and in
• Color, name, description and files for every profile
• Pin important profiles and reorder them by dragging
• Switch profiles with one tap right from the browser

PERSONAL ACCOUNT
• Passwords per profile: save after sign-in, autofill, choose an account
• Passwords are encrypted with an Android Keystore key and never leave the phone
• Showing a password requires your fingerprint or PIN
• Tab sets: save your open tabs and reopen them with one tap
• Backup to a file protected by your password — to move to a new phone

NOTES AND LIBRARY
• Notes from selected text on any page
• Saved pages for reading offline (PDF)
• Favorites with folders
• Reader mode: just the article text

DESKTOP-CLASS DOWNLOADS
• Percent and time left, cancel, a notification when done
• Filters: images, audio, video, documents
• PDFs, photos and text open right inside Ось
• Download and upload permissions for each site

PRIVACY AND SECURITY
• Blocks trackers, cryptominers and fingerprinters
• Warnings about phishing and malware sites
• Unlock the app with fingerprint or PIN
• Block screenshots and screen recording (optional)
• Incognito: no history, cookies or passwords
• HTTPS-only mode (optional)
• Site permissions: camera, microphone, location, notifications, pop-ups
• DuckDuckGo search by default; pick another engine anytime

EVERYDAY COMFORT
• Menu and address bar you already know: suggestions from history, frequent sites
• Multiple tabs and tab groups in every profile
• Find in page, desktop site, full screen, zoom
• Site notifications labeled with the profile name
• Quick search on the home screen
• A greeting and a positive thought every time you open Ось
• Usage statistics just for you, on your phone
• 10 interface languages and 6 themes, including dark and OLED
• Works on phones and tablets

A TRUSTED ENGINE
Ось is built on Mozilla GeckoView — the engine behind Firefox. Modern sites, web apps and video work the way they should.

FOR BUSINESS
Made for entrepreneurs, managers, marketers, freelancers and anyone who runs several accounts: clients, projects and personal life never mix.
