Privacy policy of the «Ось» (Os) app

Updated: October 25, 2026 · Русский

Ось is a business browser for Android with isolated profiles. Its core principle: your data stays on your device. This page explains what is processed and how it is protected.

Data the developer collects

None. The app has no servers of its own, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The developer does not receive, store or share your personal data, history, passwords, notes or files.

What is stored only on your device

  • Cookies, sign-ins and site data — separately for each profile.
  • Profiles (names, descriptions, profile files), open tabs, tab sets and groups.
  • Browsing history, favorites, notes, saved pages and the list of downloads.
  • Passwords — encrypted with a key from the Android Keystore (AES-256-GCM); the key never leaves the device. Showing or copying a password requires your fingerprint or the phone's PIN.
  • Settings and usage statistics (counted on the device and never sent anywhere).

You can delete all of this in the app (Clear data, Sign out and clear for a profile, deleting passwords and tab sets in the Personal account) or by uninstalling the app.

Network connections

  • The app connects to the websites you open yourself. Data sent to those sites is governed by their own policies.
  • Searches typed in the address bar go to the search engine you choose (DuckDuckGo by default; can be changed in Settings).
  • Phishing and malware protection (Safe Browsing) in the built-in Mozilla GeckoView engine checks addresses against lists of dangerous sites using only short anonymous hash prefixes, not the addresses themselves and not your data.
  • Address bar suggestions and frequent-site tiles come only from your history and favorites on the device, with no network requests; site icons are not downloaded.

Backup

If you choose to, the Personal account saves a copy of a profile (passwords, tab sets, favorites, notes) to a file in a place you pick. The file is encrypted with a password you set (PBKDF2-SHA256 and AES-256-GCM) and cannot be opened without it. The developer has no access to it.

Permissions

  • Internet — to open websites.
  • Notifications — for notifications from sites you allowed, and download progress.
  • Install apps — only to open an APK file you downloaded, when you tap it.
  • Camera, microphone, location — only when a site asks and you confirm (for example a video call). You can revoke it in Site permissions.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) — to save downloads to the Downloads folder.

Protection

The app can be locked with the device fingerprint or PIN. You can block screenshots and screen recording and hide the content in recent apps. Incognito tabs keep no history, cookies or passwords.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not designed for children. We collect no data from children (or anyone else).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with its update date.
